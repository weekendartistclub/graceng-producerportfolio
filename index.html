Urbanscapes

Arts Curator & Programme Manager · 2018

As Curator and Program Manager for the Arts segment at Urbanscapes, I developed a well-rounded multidisciplinary programme designed to challenge, engage and include. We introduced site-specific dance and theatre to the public, created space for environmental and educational content, and featured performances by underprivileged youth, expanding what arts programming could be in a public festival context.
