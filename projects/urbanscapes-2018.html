Urbanscapes 2018

Arts Curation / Festival Programming

Year
2018
Role
Arts Curator & Programme Manager

Overview

A multidisciplinary programme of site-specific dance and theatre, experimental work, participatory experiences, environmental education and inclusive cultural programming.

Creative Vision

Interdisciplinary collaboration, accessibility, artistic experimentation, audience engagement, and opportunities for underserved communities.

Contribution

  • Curated the Arts segment
  • Managed the programme
  • Developed a multidisciplinary line-up spanning performance, participation and environmental education

Outcomes

  • New interdisciplinary collaborations emerged within the local arts community as a direct result of creative groups working together through Urbanscapes.
  • The festival achieved record-breaking attendance of 76,000, more than doubling the previous year's attendance of 32,000.
