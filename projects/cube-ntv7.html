Cube / Ntv7

Television / Original Content Development

CUBE
Year
2010
Role
Original Concept Creator · Co-Host

Overview

Cube was a youth-oriented Malaysian television talk show developed from an original idea by Grace, broadcast on Ntv7.

Creative Vision

Inspired by conversations young Malaysians were having online, Grace conceived a format that would bring everyday young people together to discuss contemporary social issues.

Grace's Contribution

  • Created the original concept
  • Co-hosted the programme
